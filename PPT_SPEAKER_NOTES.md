# AI Visual Trend Dashboard PPT Speaker Notes

## Slide 1: Title
Introduce the project as an interactive dashboard that studies AI-generated visual culture through prompt data, tool comparison, representative works, and forecasts.

## Slide 2: Problem Framing
Explain that AI visual trends are difficult to understand from numbers alone. The dashboard is designed for designers, art directors, students, and creators who need both analytics and visual examples.

## Slide 3: Data Credibility
Clarify the data boundary honestly: the current dataset is semi-real and self-contained, but it follows a Kaggle-ready schema. Emphasize data cleaning, aggregation, and replaceability.

## Slide 4: Key Insight
Present the strongest insight: Anime leads 2024 sample volume, Cyberpunk remains strong, and AI Cinematic Storyboard shows the movement toward narrative and video-oriented creation.

## Slide 5: Product Experience
Show how the website is interactive: sidebar filters, clickable Plotly charts, random exploration, cached loading, and a unified dark visual theme.

## Slide 6: Representative Works
Explain why image examples matter. They turn abstract style labels into visible evidence and make the dashboard more convincing for creative users.

## Slide 7: AI Toolchain Benchmark
Explain that the project does not simply rank tools. It maps each tool to a production role: art direction, rapid ideation, custom workflow, commercial design, and motion/video.

## Slide 8: Unique Features
Introduce the creative direction generator, moodboard preview, use-case recommendation, and motion previews. These make the project more distinctive than a standard data dashboard.

## Slide 9: Technical Implementation
Highlight code quality: modular functions, cached CSV loading, friendly error handling, local assets, requirements file, README, and deployment readiness.

## Slide 10: Conclusion
Close with the core argument: AI visual culture is moving from style imitation toward creative direction. Mention next steps: real Kaggle prompt export, Streamlit Cloud deployment, and a short demo video.
